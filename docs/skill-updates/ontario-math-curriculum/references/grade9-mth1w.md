# Grade 9 — MTH1W (De-streamed, 2021)

## What changed

MTH1W replaced three courses starting September 2021: MPM1D (Grade 9 academic), MFM1P (Grade 9 applied), and MPM1H (applied-to-academic transfer). It is built on the old MPM1D (academic) content.

Old MPM1D strands: Number Sense and Algebra; Linear Relations; Analytic Geometry; Measurement and Geometry. MFM1P had the same minus Analytic Geometry.

MTH1W changes this three ways:
- **Reorganized** into the elementary A-F strand structure.
- **New content:** coding, mathematical modelling, data, financial literacy, number-set theory (density/infinity/limit), graphing inequalities, transformations of lines, and cultural-history/research expectations (B1.1, C1.1, E1.1).
- **Supplementary learning vs. old MPM1D:** the ministry's MPM2D addendum notes MTH1W students bring extra learning, e.g. solving linear systems by comparison and identifying regions defined by inequalities.

## Strand structure

- **Strand AA (SEL):** taught throughout, not evaluated.
- **Strand A (Mathematical Thinking and Making Connections):** evaluated through strands B-F.

## B. Number

- B1.1 research a number concept's cultural history
- B1.2 subsets of the number system
- B1.3 density, infinity and limit
- B2.1 how the sign and size of an exponent affect a power; scientific notation
- B2.2 exponent laws to simplify numeric and algebraic expressions
- B3.1 integers for location, direction and change
- B3.2 unit fractions and measuring tools
- B3.3 how signs affect ratios, rates, fractions and decimals
- B3.4 operations with positive and negative fractions and mixed numbers
- B3.5 rates, percentages and proportions

## C. Algebra

- C1.1 cultural history of an algebraic concept
- C1.2 create expressions that generalize relationships
- C1.3 identify equivalent expressions
- C1.4 simplify expressions
- C1.5 create and solve equations and verify solutions
- C2.1 code with variables, parameters, equations and inequalities
- C2.2 decompose situations into computational steps
- C2.3 read and alter code
- C3.1 linear vs. non-linear rates of change
- C3.2 represent linear relations; rate of change and initial value
- C3.3 compare two lines y = ax + b and interpret their intersection
- C4.1 characteristics of linear vs. non-linear relations
- C4.2 graph x = k, y = k, x + y = k, x − y = k, ax + by = k, xy = k and the related inequalities
- C4.3 translate, reflect and rotate y = ax
- C4.4 equations of lines from graphs and tables (slope and y-intercept)

## D. Data and Modelling

- D1.1 implications of large data
- D1.2 single-variable analysis with quartiles and box plots
- D1.3 scatter plots and regression models using technology
- D2.1-D2.5 the full modelling cycle: question of interest, plan and assumptions, analysis, report on fit and limitations

## E. Geometry and Measurement

- E1.1 cultural history of a geometric concept or measurement system
- E1.2 designs using circle and triangle properties
- E1.3 unit conversions within and between measurement systems
- E1.4 how changing dimensions affects perimeter, area, surface area and volume
- E1.5 Pythagorean relationship in composite shapes
- E1.6 prism-pyramid and cylinder-cone volume relationships

## F. Financial Literacy

- F1.1 past or current financial situations
- F1.2 appreciation and depreciation graphs
- F1.3 how interest rates, borrowing time, interest method and down payment affect cost
- F1.4 modify budgets

## Vocabulary

natural numbers, whole numbers, integers, rational and irrational numbers, density, infinity, limit, scientific notation, exponent laws, equivalent expression, parameter, rate of change, initial value, slope, y-intercept, point of intersection, quartile, box plot, regression, line of best fit, mathematical model, assumption, appreciation, depreciation, down payment.

## EQAO Grade 9 test — item-format reference

- Online, multi-stage computer-adaptive: Stage 1 performance routes the student to an easier or harder Stage 2.
- Assesses strands B-F. Does **not** assess Communication or the SEL strand.
- Every item tagged Knowledge and Understanding (KU), Application (AP) or Thinking (TH) — mirror this tagging in an app's question bank.
- Released item formats: multi-select ("select the TWO subsets"), fill-in-the-blank selection, drag-and-drop expression matching.
- Counts for 20% of the MTH1W course mark (2026-27 weighting).

# Grade 10 — MPM2D (Academic) vs MFM2P (Applied)

2005 curriculum documents, still in force, with small 2022 addenda.

## MPM2D — Principles of Mathematics (Academic)

**Quadratic Relations**
- A1: collect data; parabola with constant second differences; vertex, axis of symmetry, zeros, max/min; compare y = x² with y = 2^x; zero and negative exponents.
- A2: roles of a, h, k in y = a(x − h)² + k; sketch by hand; find the equation from a graph.
- A3: expand; factor common factors, trinomials (including a ≠ 1) and differences of squares; link factors to zeros; interpret real and non-real roots; complete the square (no fractions); develop the quadratic formula; solve by factoring, formula or graphing.
- A4: max/min and projectile problems.

**Analytic Geometry**
- B1: solve linear systems by substitution and elimination. 2022 addendum adds: slopes of parallel and perpendicular lines; develop m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); convert among y = mx + b, Ax + By + C = 0 and Ax + By = D.
- B2: midpoint and length formulas; circle x² + y² = r²; right bisector; distance from a point to a line.
- B3: verify properties of geometric figures (medians, midsegments, diagonals).

**Trigonometry**
- C1: similar triangles; similarity vs. congruence.
- C2: sine, cosine, tangent and the Pythagorean theorem.
- C3: sine law and cosine law in acute triangles.

## MFM2P — Foundations of Mathematics (Applied)

**Measurement and Trigonometry**
- A1: similar triangles by proportional reasoning.
- A2: primary trig ratios, plus trig in an occupation.
- A3: imperial system (lumber, carpet, concrete), imperial ↔ metric conversions, pyramid surface area, surface area and volume of composite solids, develop the sphere volume formula.

**Modelling Linear Relations**
- B1: first-degree equations including fractional coefficients; rearrange formulas; Ax + By + C = 0 → y = mx + b.
- B2: slope as rise/run; meaning of m and b; special cases x = a, y = b; graph lines; find equations.
- B3: point of intersection; systems with integer coefficients.

**Quadratic Relations**
- C1: expand binomial products; common factoring; factor only x² + bx + c and x² − a².
- C2: parabola features; standard form vs. factored form.
- C3: interpret graphs, including technology-generated graphs.

## Key depth differences (MFM2P has none of these)

- **Quadratics:** no completing the square, no quadratic formula, no a ≠ 1 factoring, no vertex-form transformations.
- **Trigonometry:** no sine or cosine law.
- **Geometry:** no midpoint, length or circle analytic geometry.
- **Measurement:** MFM2P instead adds imperial measurement and 3-D measurement (not in MPM2D).

## Vocabulary

parabola, vertex, axis of symmetry, zeros/roots, second differences, vertex/standard/factored form, completing the square, substitution/elimination, midpoint, perpendicular bisector, SOH-CAH-TOA, angle of elevation/depression, sine law, cosine law, imperial units.

# Grade 5: Ontario Curriculum, Grades 1-8: Mathematics, 2020

No course code (elementary). Strands A-F.

**Strand A: SEL skills and the mathematical processes.** Not assessed or reported since 2021-22; use for reflection prompts only.

## B. Number

**B1 Number Sense**
- B1.1 read, represent, compose and decompose whole numbers up to and including 100 000; describe everyday uses
- B1.2 compare and order whole numbers up to 100 000
- B1.3 represent equivalent fractions from halves to twelfths, including improper fractions and mixed numbers
- B1.4 compare and order fractions from halves to twelfths, including improper fractions and mixed numbers
- B1.5 read, represent, compare and order decimal numbers up to hundredths
- B1.6 round decimal numbers to the nearest tenth
- B1.7 relationships and equivalences among fractions, decimals up to hundredths, and whole-number percents

**B2 Operations**
- B2.1 properties of operations and relationships between operations; multi-step problems with whole numbers and decimals; check calculations
- B2.2 recall multiplication facts 0 × 0 to 12 × 12 and related division facts
- B2.3 mental math: multiply whole numbers by 0.1 and 0.01; estimate sums and differences of decimals to hundredths
- B2.4 add and subtract whole numbers (sums to 100 000) and decimals to hundredths
- B2.5 add and subtract fractions with like denominators
- B2.6 two-digit × two-digit multiplication using the area model and algorithms, connecting the two
- B2.7 three-digit ÷ two-digit division using the area model and algorithms; express remainders appropriately
- B2.8 multiply and divide one-digit whole numbers by unit fractions
- B2.9 represent and create equivalent ratios and rates

## C. Algebra

**C1 Patterns and Relationships**
- C1.1 identify and describe repeating, growing and shrinking patterns, including real-life patterns
- C1.2 create and translate growing and shrinking patterns among representations, including tables of values and graphs
- C1.3 determine pattern rules; extend, predict and find missing elements
- C1.4 patterns showing relationships among whole numbers and decimal tenths and hundredths

**C2 Equations and Inequalities**
- C2.1 translate among words, algebraic expressions and visual representations of equivalent relationships
- C2.2 evaluate algebraic expressions involving whole numbers
- C2.3 solve equations with whole numbers up to 100 and verify solutions
- C2.4 solve one-operation inequalities with whole numbers up to 50; verify and graph solutions

**C3 Coding**
- C3.1 write and execute code, including conditional statements and other control structures
- C3.2 read and alter code with conditionals and control structures; describe how changes affect outcomes

**C4 Mathematical Modelling:** apply the modelling process to real-life situations (no specific expectations; assessed through other strands).

## D. Data

**D1 Data Literacy**
- D1.1 why sampling technique matters for a representative sample
- D1.2 collect data with appropriate sampling; organize in relative-frequency tables
- D1.3 choose graphs including stacked-bar graphs; proper sources, titles, labels and scales; justify choice
- D1.4 create an infographic using relative-frequency tables and stacked-bar graphs
- D1.5 mean, median and mode(s) for whole-number and decimal data sets; what each indicates
- D1.6 analyse data, including stacked-bar and misleading graphs; draw conclusions and make arguments

**D2 Probability**
- D2.1 express probability as fractions on a probability line; make predictions
- D2.2 determine and compare theoretical and experimental probability of an event

## E. Spatial Sense

**E1 Geometric and Spatial Reasoning**
- E1.1 geometric properties of triangles; construct triangles from side or angle measures
- E1.2 identify and construct congruent triangles, rectangles and parallelograms
- E1.3 draw top, front and side views; match drawings to objects
- E1.4 plot and read coordinates in the first quadrant with various scales; describe translations between points
- E1.5 translations, reflections and rotations up to 180° on a grid; predict results

**E2 Measurement**
- E2.1 metric units to estimate and measure length, area, mass and capacity
- E2.2 convert larger metric units into smaller ones; base-ten relationships
- E2.3 compare angles by matching and with non-standard units
- E2.4 how protractors work; measure and construct angles to 180°; benchmark angles
- E2.5 develop area formulas for parallelograms and triangles from rectangles; solve problems
- E2.6 same area, different perimeters

## F. Financial Literacy

- F1.1 ways money is transferred among individuals, organizations and businesses
- F1.2 cost of transactions with multiple items, including sales tax
- F1.3 design basic budgets for earning and spending scenarios
- F1.4 credit and debt, and their effect on financial decisions
- F1.5 unit rates; identify best value
- F1.6 types of taxes collected by each level of government in Canada and how revenue funds services

## Vocabulary

place value, improper fraction, mixed number, equivalent fraction, hundredths, percent, area model, remainder, unit fraction, ratio, rate, expression, equation, inequality, conditional statement, sample, relative frequency, stacked-bar graph, mean, median, mode, probability line, congruent, first quadrant, translation, reflection, rotation, protractor, benchmark angle, parallelogram, sales tax, credit, debt, unit rate.

## Notes for app sequencing

- Grade 5 is where fractions, decimals and percents first connect (B1.7); this feeds Grade 6 B1.6 and B2.5.
- Fraction addition is like denominators only (B2.5); unlike denominators arrive in Grade 6.
- Coordinates are first quadrant only; all four quadrants arrive in Grade 6.
- Inequalities are one operation, whole numbers to 50.

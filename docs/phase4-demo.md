# Phase 4 demo script (2.3.0): motivation and caregivers

Time: about 15 minutes. You need a caregiver account with a Grade 5 or 6 student and an approved device.

1. **Earn XP.** Sign in as the student and practice a few questions. After each answer you see what you earned (+XP with the reasons), and the strip at the top shows your level, XP bar and day streak. A wrong answer still earns a small "good try" reward; XP never goes down.
2. **Badges and levels.** Your first answer earns "First steps". Keep going to see level-ups and more badges. Open **My rewards** to see every badge, how to earn the locked ones, and your streak and freezes.
3. **Wear it.** On My rewards, pick an avatar and a colour palette. Some are locked until a level or badge. (To try them all quickly, a developer can raise the XP in the database.) With high contrast on, palettes are paused so colours stay plain.
4. **Streaks.** Practice on two days in a row to grow the streak. Miss exactly one day and a streak freeze (earned every seventh day) keeps it safe; miss more and it starts again.
5. **Goals (caregiver).** Open **Progress and goals** for the student. Add a goal such as "Movie night" for 500 XP. The student sees a progress bar. When it is reached, the student is told and you press **Mark as done** after giving the reward.
6. **Progress dashboard.** The same page shows the last 14 days, accuracy, time, progress by topic, where a little help could count, and reviews due. "Show as a table" gives the same chart as text.
7. **Printable worksheet.** On that page choose a topic, difficulty and number of questions, then **Make worksheet**. Print it, or choose Save as PDF. The answer key prints on its own page, and you can leave it out.
8. **Daily goal and break.** In the caregiver settings set a daily goal and a session limit (a student can also have their own). Past the limit a session ends with a friendly break message, and a new one waits 20 minutes.
9. **Weekly email.** Turn on the weekly email in the caregiver settings. On Monday from 07:30 (family time) you get one summary in English or French.
10. **Privacy.** Export and delete both include rewards.

What is not in this phase: Grades 7 to 12 content, the PWA and offline practice (Phase 5).

# Phase 2 demo script (2.1.0): the learning experience

Time: about 15 minutes. You need a caregiver account with a student in Grade 5 or 6 and an approved device (see the Phase 1 demo).

1. **Sign in as the student** (`/app/student`). You land on the learning home: choose a **grade**, then **topics** (or "Mix it up"). Strands without content yet say "coming soon".
2. **Learn it first.** Open any topic's lesson with the "Show me how" link on a question, or from the skill map. A lesson has plain-language definitions with pictures, three worked examples that build in difficulty (the pictures animate), and "Try one with me" with hints.
3. **Practise.** Pick Grade 5, topic B (Number), and Start practice. Answer a few questions:
   - Question types you will meet: number entry, fraction entry, mixed numbers, multiple choice, multi-select, put-in-order, matching, and tap-the-grid point plotting.
   - Open the **math keyboard** on a number question (every key is 44 px or larger and has a spoken label). A live KaTeX preview shows what you typed.
   - Open the **scratchpad** beside the question and draw. It is never graded or saved.
4. **Hints.** Press "Hint" up to three times: a nudge, then a worked step, then the full worked example. Hints reduce the credit a little and never block you.
5. **Feedback.** Answer one wrongly on purpose. The message is kind, names the likely misconception ("It looks like the denominators were added..."), shows the step where it changed and the correct answer, and offers "Try a similar one". A right answer gets short, varied praise, sometimes naming the skill you used.
6. **Struggle and recover.** Keep missing the same skill. You will see, in order: a gentler question (one tier down), the skill broken into single-step micro-skills, then a short lesson on the weakest prerequisite, then the build back up (micro-skill, combined skill, original tier, one tier at a time).
7. **Placement.** On the home page choose "Quick check (placement)". It asks at most eight questions, adapts as you go, and ends with "All set!". Practice then starts at the right level.
8. **Skill map.** Open "Skill map" for Grade 6: nodes show mastered, in progress, needs review, not started, "recommended first" (a prerequisite is still weak; practice is never blocked) and coming soon. Tap a node for details and "Practise this".
9. **Voice (optional).** Settings -> Read aloud (off by default), turn on "Read questions and feedback automatically", and adjust the speed. Math is read as words ("three fourths", "x squared"), never symbol by symbol, and the spoken words also appear as a caption. Switch to Français and it reads with a fr-CA voice.
10. **Accessibility.** Settings -> high contrast, dyslexia-friendly font (OpenDyslexic), reduce motion. The whole flow works by keyboard alone.
11. **Spaced review.** After a skill is mastered it returns after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days; a slip sends it back to practice.

What is not in this phase: content beyond the Grade 5-6 number, fractions, decimals and coordinates seed set (Phase 3 builds it out grade by grade), the Claude-generated item pipeline and review queue (Phase 3), sliders, draggable parabolas, protractor, probability simulators and code-snippet items (Phase 3-5), rewards and themes (Phase 4), the PWA and offline practice (Phase 5).

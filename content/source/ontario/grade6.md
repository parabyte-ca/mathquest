# Grade 6 — Ontario Curriculum, Grades 1-8: Mathematics, 2020

No course code (elementary). Strands A-F.

**Strand A — SEL skills and the mathematical processes.** Applied throughout; not a question bank. Use for reflection prompts.

## B. Number

**B1 Number Sense**
- B1.1 whole numbers up to 1,000,000
- B1.2 read and represent integers on horizontal and vertical number lines
- B1.3 compare and order integers, decimals and fractions, separately and in combination
- B1.4 decimals to thousandths
- B1.5 round terminating and repeating decimals to the nearest tenth, hundredth or whole number
- B1.6 equivalences between fractions and decimals up to thousandths

**B2 Operations**
- B2.1 properties of operations; multi-step problems with whole numbers, decimals, fractions, ratios, rates and whole-number percents
- B2.2 divisibility rules for 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10
- B2.3 mental math for 1%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 25%, 50%
- B2.4 add and subtract whole numbers and decimals
- B2.5 add and subtract fractions with like and unlike denominators
- B2.6 prime factorization using factor trees
- B2.7 three-digit whole number × decimal tenths
- B2.8 three-digit whole number ÷ decimal tenths
- B2.9 whole number × proper fraction
- B2.10 whole number ÷ proper fraction
- B2.11 decimals to thousandths ÷ whole numbers up to 10
- B2.12 ratios, percents and rates

## C. Algebra

**C1 Patterns**
- C1.1 repeating, growing and shrinking patterns; identify which growing patterns are linear
- C1.2 translate patterns among tables, graphs, and (for linear patterns) expressions/equations
- C1.3 pattern rules, predictions, missing elements; solve for unknowns in linear patterns
- C1.4 patterns among whole numbers and decimals

**C2 Equations and Inequalities**
- C2.1 add monomials of degree 1 with whole numbers, using tools
- C2.2 evaluate expressions with whole numbers and decimal tenths
- C2.3 solve multi-term equations with whole numbers and verify solutions
- C2.4 inequalities

**C3 Coding:** write and alter code, including code with conditional statements and other control structures.

**C4 Mathematical Modelling:** the modelling process, applied across strands.

## D. Data

- D1.1 discrete vs. continuous data
- D1.2 collect and organize data, including using intervals
- D1.3 choose graphs, including histograms and broken-line graphs
- D1.4 infographics
- D1.5 range and measures of central tendency
- D1.6 analyse data, including misleading graphs
- D2.1 express probability as fractions, decimals and percents on a probability line
- D2.2 theoretical vs. experimental probability of two independent events

## E. Spatial Sense

- E1.1 properties of quadrilaterals, including diagonals and symmetry
- E1.2 build 3-D objects from top, front and side views
- E1.3 coordinates in all four quadrants
- E1.4 combinations of translations, reflections and rotations up to 360°
- E2.1 metric conversions
- E2.2 protractor use up to 360°
- E2.3 supplementary, complementary, opposite, interior and exterior angles
- E2.4 area of trapezoids, rhombuses, kites and composite polygons
- E2.5 nets
- E2.6 surface area of prisms and pyramids

## F. Financial Literacy

- F1.1 payment methods
- F1.2 financial goals
- F1.3 factors that help or interfere with reaching goals
- F1.4 interest rates and fees on accounts and loans
- F1.5 trading, lending, borrowing, donating

## Vocabulary

integer, repeating decimal, prime factorization, factor tree, divisibility, ratio, rate, percent, monomial, variable, linear pattern, histogram, broken-line graph, discrete/continuous data, four quadrants, supplementary/complementary angle, net, surface area, interest rate.

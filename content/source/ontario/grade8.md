# Grade 8 — Ontario Curriculum, Grades 1-8: Mathematics, 2020

No course code (elementary). Strands A-F.

## B. Number

- B1.1 very large and very small numbers; scientific notation
- B1.2 real numbers (rational and irrational)
- B1.3 estimate and calculate square roots
- B1.4 percents above 100% and below 1%
- B2.1 order of operations with rational numbers
- B2.2 recall common squares and square roots
- B2.3 multiply and divide by powers of ten mentally
- B2.4 add and subtract integers
- B2.5 add and subtract fractions
- B2.6 multiply and divide fractions by fractions, whole numbers and mixed numbers
- B2.7 multiply and divide integers
- B2.8 compare proportional situations and find unknown values

## C. Algebra

- C1.1 compare linear growing AND shrinking patterns by rate and initial value
- C1.2 represent patterns with rational numbers
- C1.3 pattern rules and solving for unknowns
- C1.4 patterns among rational numbers
- C2.1 add and subtract monomials; add degree-1 binomials with integers
- C2.2 evaluate expressions with rational numbers
- C2.3 multi-term equations with integers and decimals
- C2.4 solve, verify and graph inequalities with integers
- C3 code that analyses data to inform decisions
- C4 modelling

## D. Data

- D1.1 one-variable vs. two-variable data
- D1.2 two-variable tables of values
- D1.3 scatter plots
- D1.4 infographics
- D1.5 describe correlation as strong, weak, none, positive or negative, including outliers
- D1.6 analysis
- D2.1 probability using Venn and tree diagrams
- D2.2 multiple independent and dependent events

## E. Spatial Sense

- E1.1 tessellations
- E1.2 build models from views
- E1.3 scale drawings
- E1.4 all four transformations, including dilations, on the Cartesian plane
- E2.1 mega/giga/tera and micro/nano/pico units in exponential notation
- E2.2 angle properties of intersecting and parallel lines and polygons
- E2.3 perimeter, area, volume and surface area of composite shapes
- E2.4 Pythagorean theorem

## F. Financial Literacy

- F1.1 payment methods across currencies
- F1.2 long-term financial plan including taxes
- F1.3 balanced budgets
- F1.4 growth of simple vs. compound interest using digital tools
- F1.5 sales, loyalty and incentive programs
- F1.6 compare credit cards and consumer contracts

## Vocabulary

scientific notation, irrational number, real number system, binomial, correlation, outlier, scatter plot, tree diagram, tessellation, scale factor, Pythagorean theorem, hypotenuse, transversal, compound interest, credit card APR.
